# Compliance Profile

The single place CareerOS reads **who you certify to and under what rules**. The `log-activity` and `generate-weekly-cert` commands, the compliance pack and every document in this section use these values instead of assuming an agency.

Fill this in from your claim notice and your agency's published rules. Delete the brackets. Leave a value as `[unknown]` rather than guessing; commands will ask you instead of assuming.

If you are not claiming unemployment benefits, you can ignore this whole section.
